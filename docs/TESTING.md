# Needs device testing

- [ ] **Spotify startup fallback.** Use an account that sees audio key refusals. Check that a waiting track falls back promptly, then repeat on a second track and confirm an already playing track stays on its current route.
- [ ] **Live radio.** Search and play an HTTPS station with each music source selected. Check favorites, recents, directory failure and the notice for unencrypted stations. Restart and confirm the live station does not replace the previously saved music queue.
- [ ] **Hidden content.** Hide a song and ban an artist, then check manual queues, song radio, autoplay and continuation pages. Unhide and unban them in Settings and check that they can appear again.
- [ ] **On-device playlists.** Create, rename and delete a playlist. Add, play and remove tracks, then restart to check persistence. Reorder pinned playlists and verify the order after restarting.
- [ ] **Playlist links.** Import multi-page Spotify and YouTube Music playlists, including a Spotify short link. Check import and skip counts, playback with the matching source, the prompt when the wrong source is selected, and inaccessible or malformed links. Use a connected Spotify Web API account where required.
- [ ] **Home filters and releases.** Switch Spotify Home among the feed filters and any available subfilters. Check loading, empty, error and retry behavior. Follow or unfollow an artist, refresh releases and switch accounts to check account isolation and the reconnect prompt.
- [ ] **Listening recap.** Check month, year and all-time totals after playing streamed and downloaded tracks. Check top tracks, albums, source totals, an empty period and available account history.
- [ ] **Lyrics and haptics.** Check Genius matching, missing lyrics and the external Genius link. Toggle haptics and lyrics keep-awake. Check playback controls and scrubbing, then confirm normal screen timeout resumes when leaving lyrics.
- [ ] **Looks and layout.** Check preset, custom and cover-derived accent colors, invalid hex input, cover rotation and each player visibility switch. Rotate a phone and resize or rotate a tablet with the player open. Check navigation, focus and the playback position.
- [ ] **Audio quality, savers and headset.** Check Spotify quality choices and newly loaded YouTube Music streams on Wi-Fi and metered networks, signed out and with Premium. Compare the player format and bitrate readout. Toggle the savers under low battery, Android Battery Saver and cellular conditions, and check preloading and artwork behavior. Restart the player during playback. Try headset next and previous on a podcast and a song.
- [ ] **YouTube Music Premium account switch.** With Premium signed in and quality set to Best available, play a track that is also available without Premium. Switch to a non-Premium account or sign out, then load and play the same track again. Verify that playback uses a stream available to the new session without reusing the previous account's stream.
- [ ] **AutoMix.** With crossfade enabled and two Spotify tracks queued, load beat analysis. Check bar snapping, cue nudges, overlap, save, reset, cache clearing and diagnostics. Check the state with crossfade off or no eligible pair.
- [ ] **Nearby Jams.** Share and join a Jam on two phones on the same network. Stop sharing, close the panel and test discovery failure and invite-link fallback.
- [ ] **Output discovery.** Use Cast devices, a Cast group and Sonos on the same network. Check discovery, matching to available Spotify Connect outputs, transfer to a matched output and behavior when it disappears.
- [ ] **Pass.** Transfer a YouTube Music sign-in between two phones on the same Wi-Fi and confirm playback on the receiver. Check wrong passphrase, expired QR, cancellation, different networks and an existing sign-in on the receiver.
- [ ] **Astral import.** Use a real encrypted export. Check wrong passphrase, existing sign-ins, compatible preferences, local playlists with and without media permission, and history import with matching and different Spotify accounts.
- [ ] **Extensions.** Browse and refresh the catalog, then install and update an available package. Try a local ZIP and an HTTPS URL, including a wrong checksum. Check enable, disable, status and removal after restarting the app.
- [ ] **Modules.** Open from Settings and the optional tab. Check BackMusic artwork, spectrum and waveform with supported playback, missing artwork, unsupported playback and animations disabled in Android.
- [ ] **Animated artwork.** Check opt-in default, correct and ambiguous release matches, Apple motion hosts and HLS redirects, still-art fallback, and behavior in saver mode and with Android animations disabled.
- [ ] **Android Auto.** On a paired host, browse library, playlists, recents and downloads online and offline. Try voice search through Google Assistant, playback, like, shuffle and radio actions, including legacy custom actions. Switch accounts and check that the old account's browse results are cleared.
- [ ] **Accessibility and language.** Walk through the new screens with TalkBack and keyboard focus. Check target sizes, control labels and every new flow in English and Italian, including error and empty states.
